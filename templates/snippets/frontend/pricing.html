Can I use SmSt for Free?

Yes! SmSt Africa is free for use with just a SmSt Account. However there are limits placed in each app which can be unlocked by joining pro!

What are the payment Methods?

SmSt Africa uses Paystack to request payments from users through their mobile wallet (MPESA or Airtel Money) or through their credit card. None of the payment information is stored in our server in order to increase security for your account. Every payment step is managed directly by Paystack. Read their Terms of Conditions and Privacy Policy to understand how they process your data.

Can I get Refunds?

Yes, but only withing the first Seven (7) days after your first payment. Consequent monthly payments are not refundable.
